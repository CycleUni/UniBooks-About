## 5. Condition Grades

Sellers must choose one of four grades and attach photos of the actual book:

| Grade | Definition |
|:---:|---|
| **Like New** | Unused; cover, pages and spine in perfect shape |
| **No Notes** | Shows signs of reading but no writing, highlighting or dog-ears inside |
| **Marked** | Contains pen marks, highlighting or folds, but content is complete and readable |
| **Damaged** | Missing pages, stains, loose binding or other clear damage; must be clearly shown in photos and description |

A buyer may decline to complete a deal if the grade is inaccurate; misgrading confirmed through a report is handled under Section 10.
