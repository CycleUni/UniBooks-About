# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## 1. What is UniBooks?

UniBooks is a second-hand textbook search and matching platform designed for university students in Taiwan. It uses ISBN-based book records so all sellers of the same title appear on one listings, making it simple to search, compare prices, and read condition details.

## 2. Is it free?

Yes. Campus meetup transactions in the same school have zero cost. The website collects no listing fee, no transaction fee, no hidden costs. Only when both parties voluntarily choose the shipping-plus-escrow service do we apply a single 4-5% service fee on the paid total — transparent at checkout.

## 3. Can I browse without logging in?

Yes. Search results and listing pages are publicly accessible without an account. Logging in with your .edu.tw email only unlocks messaging, publishing a listing and waitlist notification.

## 4. Why .edu.tw authentication?

Valid .edu.tw ensures everyone who trades or communicates is an actual Taiwanese university student, keeping the trust healthy.

## 5. Can I still use the account after graduation?

Yes. Your account continues to work but carries Alumn titles. Some on‑campus only features may become limited.

## 6. Do I need to re-verify every semester?

Yes. Semester re‑verification confirms your current student status; a clean student community is trust the advertisement business chain.

## 7. How to sell a book?

Visit the selling page, enter the ISBN → our system retrieves the book's details (title, cover). Confirm the condition: pick the appropriate subject, upload real photos and set the price (including 0 if want to give). Then push submit.

## 8. Can I give away books for free?

Yes. Turning the price to 0.

## 9. How to manage pricing?

Look at comparisons from other ex-sellers and match your condition. Normally 30-70% of starting price.

## 10. What is Waitlist?

If a book you were looking for not yet listed, click the "Notify Me" button. When you an sellers publishes that book — you receive an alert email instantly.

## 11. What is “N people waiting”?

Number of waiters for the book indicates its popularity.

## 12. How to contact a seller?

Click the “Contact Seller” button from book page.

## 13. Safety?

On-campus meet, we recommend public university spaces. For cross‑campus, activate with escrow; buyer has 72 hours verification.

## 14. Report a problem?

Click "Report" on the book page to flag suspicious listings; our support reviews.

## 15. Support contact?

Official support email — openings soon to be announced on our home pages.
