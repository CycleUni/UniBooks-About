# The Waiting List

Not seeing the book you need? Set a notification and let that platform track it.

## What Is the Waitlist?

UniBooks's waitlist is your subscription for any book that hasn't yet been listed for sale. Click "Notify Me" to register, and when that book appears, you'll be among the first to know.

## How to Subscribe

1. **Find the book** — search by ISBN, title, or course name.
2. **Click "Notify Me"** — one tap for open subscription.
3. **Confirm** — the system tracks the book navigation for sale, no more monitoring on your side.

No limit — subscribe to as many books as you like.

## N People Waiting

Each book's detail page displays how many subscribers are watching. That number is a **green incentive signal** to all potential sellers telling them: "There's real demand here; put your book up." More waiters = more likely a student will list it.

## Receiving a no-arrival notification

The instant new listing appears for a book on your waitlist, you receive toward the `.edu.tw` email:
- Book title
- Condition (like new / good / used / worn)
- Price
- Seller school
- One-click to the purchase page

> You don't need to scan the app — suitable email alerts and. There's nothing for us. Imaginary wanted titles disappear fast.

## Managing Your Subscriptions

Go to **Personal Page** → **My Subscriptions** to view, add, or prune what you're tracking.

## After You Got Notified

- **Don't wait one hour** — notifications are broadcast to everyone. Act.
- **Condition doesn't satisfy?** Skip the listing and wait for the next.
- **Same campus works better** — a seller from your school means you can meet quickly — the time faster.

UniBooks informs everyone on the waitlist equally, but the first mover reserves the book.
